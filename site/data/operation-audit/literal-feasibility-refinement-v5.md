# Thirteen source-bound lower endpoints and twelve conditional upper witnesses

This version preserves all prior receipts. It adds own-source p7/p17 checkpoints
4416,4440,4441,4447 and the later PR5413, and closes own-source upper witnesses for
the earlier lower-only PR4359, PR4483 and PR4533. Thirteen source constructions
now have literal-family lower bounds; twelve have conditional sufficient upper
bounds. PR4565 remains lower-only. Uncosted historical candidates remain unknown.

Each new point retains its own native word, sufficient uniform rank, compiler,
tensor power and complete field roles. The p7/p17 bundle reconstructs profiles
from each historical controller and the actual changed helper catalogs. Its
conservative eight-real-ADD lower is separate from the p16 raw scalar count.
PR5413 uses its own 32-role boundary and bank ADD census, giving c38304; the
earlier scalar count630412 is not transferred to this changed word. PR4483 and
PR4533 use their own raw, unsplit convergence compilers rather than a productive
floor borrowed from a later construction. Each has fresh source checks, numerical
precision/stock guards, seven cost rows and an independent AI review receipt.

All twelve upper witnesses share the external early protocol kappa_eff=1/500,
delta=1/10000, A=10001 and p=256*ceil(log2 n). Each retains its actual native
uniform saving. PR4359's smaller actual saving .002304 requires the explicitly
declared auxiliary chain q=.0023, a=.002301, b=.002302; the other branches use
a=.0024, b=.0025. Thus its helper funding is source-specific even though the
external comparison protocol is common. At each own original record-tail gate,
the program switches to its own independently counted original record branch.
Its source exponent is preserved, and the early effective exponent is not called
a new record. No upper is inferred merely by changing the source's exponent label.

The model and comparator remain unchanged: straight-line XOR–AND circuit work,
AND1/XORrho for rho>0, against the repaired unlimited native-profile GMP extension
`gmp-630-native-skylake-unbounded-exact-constants-v2` and the canonical counted
HvH circuit. Each lower rules out a strict win below its endpoint for every
switch policy in the declared family: unchanged comparator while inactive,
complete unreduced own native core while active. An upper exhibits a correct
family member winning at every larger input size. Neither endpoint is a claimed
actual first crossing, a benchmark, a universal algorithmic lower bound or a
claim about practical multiplication.

The active-work lower has c*2^N AND and3c*2^N XOR. The source-specific GMP
degree-eight upper yields L=2^(2^(H-3)-2^69+floor((floor(log2 c)-3)/8)), omitting
a zero offset. Every existing lower remains byte-for-byte the same expression
in this receipt. The intervals remain enormous and broad. A smaller sufficient
upper alone compares proof witnesses; a separately checked upper lying below
another family's necessary lower can order those declared family guarantees.

All native/all-column, absolute-phase, spectator, owned-erasure, compiler and
analytic/recovery contracts are retained explicitly, together with the comparator's
finite-native correctness and halting contracts. This is AI-assisted written
mathematics with reproducible exact source/type/arithmetic checks and separate
AI reviews, not a complete Lean theorem or independent human verification.
