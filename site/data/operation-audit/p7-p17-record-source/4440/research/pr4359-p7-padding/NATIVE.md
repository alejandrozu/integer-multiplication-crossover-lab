## 2. Native owner maps at ambient rank 121

The old source utility used a fixed 73-axis container. Its owner formula has
a dimension-independent extension, which is checked here at every one of
15 stages and 112 ports rather than assuming that the old container extends.

For port normal q, let b_1,...,b_8 be the actual first eight orthogonal axes
from its pinned initial idle recipe. The checker establishes

```
b_i dot b_j = delta_ij,      b_i dot q = 0.
```

At stage s>0, let e_i be the fresh coordinate axes in positions
`9+8*(s-1),...,9+8*s-1`. Put `v_i=b_i+e_i` over F2 and define

```
G_s(x) = x + sum_i (v_i dot x)*v_i.
```

The v_i are mutually orthogonal isotropic vectors. Hence G_s is orthogonal
and involutive, swaps each b_i with its corresponding fresh e_i, and fixes q.
Stage zero uses the identity. The checker verifies the complete 121-column
matrix and its involution, orthogonality and q action in every stage/port.
All coordinates outside the original nine-axis block and that stage's fresh
eight-axis block are fixed. This supplies a legal full-field coordinate map.

Write C_a for the source Gaussian factor on a nonzero binary axis a. Its
Fourier multiplier is a Z4 phase. The phase polynomial of a product of C_a
factors has linear terms modulo4 and quadratic coefficients modulo2;
higher parity terms vanish modulo4. A physical translation adds an even
linear phase. Every C_a product and translation has multiplier one at
Fourier address zero, so its constant phase is independently fixed to zero.
Comparing the stored linear and quadratic parts therefore proves the full
phase, rather than merely the polar rank.

Let `F_s` denote the positive product on the first `9+8*s` axes and let

```
D_0 = F_0*C_q^(-1),
D_s = product of positive factors on fresh block s  (s>0).
```

For each forward/backward native call the checker constructs the four
entry/exit coordinate-and-translation maps between its actual main references
and a shared spectator frame. It verifies both map directions, the exact
raw diagonal difference D_s, and the cross operator D_s*C_q^(-1) for a
forward call or D_s*C_q for a backward call. Thus the raw local contracts are

```
forward:  D_s * [[1,0],[C_q^(-1),1]],
backward: D_s * [[1,-C_q],[0,1]].
```

These are raw-input operator equalities, so they remain valid after the
controller's earlier scalar basis changes and on arbitrary correlated data.
The exact audit covers all 6,720 main contacts (15*112*4), with inverse maps.
The all-column lift replaces each coordinate by its common column block;
this is the inherited native generator substitution, not an assertion that
tensor powers distribute over addition of matrices.
