# Eight source-bound lower endpoints and four conditional upper witnesses

This version preserves versions1,2 and3 unchanged. It adds PR4359's own p3
literal-work lower, and freshly counted whole uppers for the own-source
PR4479 and PR4535 suppliers. It uses a separately reviewed common early
outer retuning of all four own-source suppliers, preserving their original
independently counted record tails. Four constructions have two-sided conditional
family intervals; PR4359,4483,4533 and4565 retain lower bounds only. No upper
is copied to a construction with unauthenticated compiler or outer parameters.

The PR4359 lower comes from its own-head rank receipt, native cover and p3
controller, with four nonzero complex ADDs giving a conservative eight real
ADDs per complete coordinate. Its source rank is N=2^276540184361 and its
own exponent is23/10000. It does not reuse the p16 word count630412. The same
fixed repaired optimized-GMP degree8 upper yields j3,q0 and the endpoint
2^(2^276540184358-2^69). Its upper remains null.

PR4479 uses its own v682 charged completion, floor21, paid cutoff17, uniform
saving, H558100005581, much smaller outer delta and finite Klog21988. PR4535
uses its own raw v1 critical-root compiler, source class/width envelope and
strict integer child descent; it does not inherit a v1024 productive floor.
Each new upper has its own source bindings, precision/stock guard, helper
pricing, seven cost rows, fine-prime capacity and exact recovery checks.
The original byte-identical native class is retained only where authenticated.
Their peer receipts are separate and hashed. No literal huge word is executed.

All four source ranks, native words and record exponents stay fixed. Each
new early branch uses the same effective outer saving1/500 and delta1/10000,
allowing a direct comparison of sufficient bounds under a common protocol.
The new extension proof rechecks each actual native uniform saving, own
stock/Clifford guard, complete seven-row count and analytic gates; changing
the labels alone would not justify these bounds. At its own independently
certified old record upper, each program switches to its unchanged record
branch. Both branches beat the same reference circuits throughout their
certified ranges; each tail retains its own original asymptotic exponent.
The JSON binds every early upper, original native count certificate and old
record-tail backup. The common early effective exponent is not relabeled as
a new record, and a proof-validity switch is never used as a necessary lower
bound. A smaller certified upper compares proof witnesses, not actual first
crossing times or practical feasibility.

All eight lower endpoints retain the same opposite comparison: the active
unreduced own literal core costs at least c*2^N AND and3c*2^N XOR; the identical
optimized-GMP reference used while inactive has each gate type at most
2^(2^72+3)*(n+1)^8. Thus, for j=floor(log2c), q=floor((j-3)/8),
L=2^(2^(H-3)-2^69+q), omitting the zero offset when q=0. These are family
lower bounds for every positive XOR tariff, independent of the switch policy.
They exclude compression, smaller suppliers, pruning the literal core,
alternative scalar lowering and a different inactive algorithm.

The upper witness establishes one family member winning at every larger size.
Combining it with the appropriate own-source lower brackets the family's
earliest attainable strict win. It does not locate one program's first
crossing, establish a hardware speedup or order actual crossing times across
PRs. The intervals remain extremely broad. Unknown uppers remain null.

The model is straight-line Boolean arithmetic work, AND1 and XORrho>0, with
public constants/routing free and all width-dependent arithmetic charged.
Native, absolute-phase, ownership, compiler and analytic/recovery contracts,
and the comparator's finite-native correctness/halting contracts, remain
conditional. Every new parameter application has its own source and review
receipt; this document does not turn those contracts into a complete theorem.
The work is AI-assisted written mathematics, exact arithmetic/source checks
and separate AI review, not Lean formalization or independent human review.
